# Canonical Spotify reconciliation validation

## Evidence and final decisions

Reconciliation now assembles typed observations from canonical application metadata, sparse overrides, local sources, persisted catalog Album/Release metadata and trusted identities. Each observation retains its origin. Local audio is optional. MusicBrainz import persists title/date/type and ordered Track title/position/duration observations in migration 0028. Withdrawn trusted identities deactivate their provider observations. Ordered program reads are batched by Album; no MusicBrainz network request is needed during evaluation. Confirmed Artist equivalence remains application-level evidence. Display credits, metadata, sources and membership are preserved.

Missing release date/type/disc/duration is neutral. Compatible known dates corroborate at their shared precision; conflicting dates retain their provenance and warning. Known release type participates, including the existing compatible EP/Single representation. Unspecified disc is neutral against disc 1; known conflicting discs/positions reject. Missing evidence does not replace required identity/program corroboration.

The explicit diagnostic previously rendered automatic song qualification without invoking automatic persistence. In addition, an existing-provider association guard lived in the later confirmation path. The diagnostic completion now applies final accepted results; all candidate identity, competition, structural and existing-association guards are exposed in the final evaluation. Current evidence is reloaded inside an immediate transaction. Stale search snapshots require re-evaluation; accepted writes are verified and committed atomically. SQL errors and even silently ignored inserts produce `AssociationPersistenceFailed`, distinct from rejection. No accepted write is silently discarded.

The diagnostic compares **KNOWN TRACK / ALBUM** with **SPOTIFY CANDIDATE** in the same field order. Unknown, agreement, normalized/compatible, warning and conflict are distinct statuses. Primary blockers remain separate from warnings and acceptance evidence. Expandable source annotations retain raw local/provider observations; program details render the actual matcher evidence. The persisted last evaluation is labeled separately. Successful completions patch Spotify Connections by canonical Track ID, preserving viewport, sort, Album filter and column widths; browsing remains provider-call-free.

### Normalization and remaining blockers

- **Untitled:** one balanced whole-title punctuation pair is comparison-only normalization. `[untitled]` and `Untitled` agree with established context; arbitrary internal brackets and semantic qualifiers remain meaningful. Real local-backed Album reconciliation connected all eight Tracks.
- **Audiotree:** `Tera Melos on Audiotree Live` becomes `Audiotree Live` only with established Artist identity and required structural evidence. This normalization is visible; the real candidate still fails because its additional Audiotree Artist credit is not established as compatible. No forced association.
- **X’ed Out:** local and MusicBrainz raw titles use U+2019 (`X’ed Out`); Spotify uses U+0027 (`X'ed Out`). General typography normalization yields `x'ed out` for both. Weird Circles and New Chlorine accept through bounded Track lookup. Album candidate discovery still returns zero, including after query typography normalization; that separate discovery limitation remains.
- **Catalog-only:** real MusicBrainz `[untitled]` import has eight saved Tracks, zero playable-source rows and eight trusted Spotify Track associations after Album reconciliation. A separate MusicBrainz X’ed Out import has twelve saved Tracks, zero local sources, and two trusted Spotify associations after explicit bounded Track searches. Library membership is preserved. Unit tests verify playlist entries resolve the same canonical Track association without rematching.
- **tsosis:** the confirmed equivalence and Blue Version (5) / Red Version (6) associations remain intact. Native search uses the already trusted provider Artist name. Hot and Bothered by Space now resolves; To Kelly Lee explicitly needs review of its older provider association. No fuzzy alias inference or overwriting was introduced.
- **Controls:** The Second Floral EP 6/6, Spiritual Bankruptcy 5/5, Hugs EP 3/3 remain resolved. Piglet — Songs remains 9/9 unresolved, and Freshman Year 16/16, including Organ Song. Version, program and Artist evidence are not weakened.
